        The Adobe PhD Fellowship recognizes exceptional doctoral students whose work
        advances the research areas at the heart of Adobe — among them generative AI,
        computer vision, natural language processing, and the modeling of human
        behavior. Fellows receive financial support for their doctoral studies,
        sustained mentorship from Adobe researchers, and the opportunity to work
        alongside our teams on ambitious, real-world problems. The program is part of
        Adobe's long-standing commitment to nurturing outstanding early-career
        researchers and deepening our partnership with the global academic community.
